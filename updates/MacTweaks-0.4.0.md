# MacTweaks 0.4.0

The first public release.

- **Window Grip**: hold a modifier and drag anywhere in a window to move or resize it.
- **Cursor Jump**: shortcuts that move the cursor between displays.
- **Tile Link**: windows that share an edge resize together.
- **Mouse & Trackpad**: three-finger trackpad click or tap and two-finger Magic Mouse click act as a
  middle click; middle-click paste of the selected text.
- Updates now arrive automatically. Check by hand with **Check for Updates…** in the menu.
